# schemas

- [Error reference schema][sokf:schema-error-reference] - The findings that smllm validate reports under one rule code, with their cause, their fix, and an example.
- [Explanation schema][sokf:schema-explanation] - Why smllm works the way it does, for a reader who wants to understand one part of smllm.
- [Front page schema][sokf:schema-front-page] - The page that the site opens on, which says who each section is for and where each reader starts.
- [How-to guide schema][sokf:schema-how-to] - One task with smllm, shown from start to finish, for a reader who knows what they want to do.
- [Reference schema][sokf:schema-reference] - A page where a reader looks up facts, such as the forms of the status line row or the rules of smllm validate.
- [Tutorial schema][sokf:schema-tutorial] - A step-by-step lesson in which a reader who is new to smllm builds one thing that works.
